# Skills — when to use a playbook

Freeform chat follows the standing harness (curiosity, learning, growth) plus
this steering. Skills are opt-in playbooks for one kind of task
(`investigate`, `review`, `qa`, `ceo`, `learn`, `compact`).

- When the task is clearly one of those jobs, follow that skill. Do not paste
  its procedure back into the standing prompt.
- `/until` and graph nodes stay on the **goal text**. Do not replace the goal
  with a memory topic.
